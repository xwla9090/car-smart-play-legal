Privacy Policy

Car Smart Play · Last updated: October 27, 2026

Car Smart Play ("the app") is a media player for content you provide yourself. We built it so that your information stays on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your sources – playlist links, playlist files and media server addresses you add are saved only on your iPhone.
  • Usernames and passwords for servers you add (Xtream, Jellyfin, Emby) are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and are sent only to the server you entered.
  • Favorites, bookmarks and settings are saved only on your device.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Location – used only on your device to check your speed, so video plays on the car screen only while the car is parked. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Local network – used to receive videos cast from other apps on the same Wi-Fi (DLNA/UPnP) and to reach media servers you add.
  • Photos – only the videos you pick are opened, to play them.
  • Screen broadcast – when you start it, your iPhone screen is sent only to the car display through your own iPhone. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Third-party content and servers

When you play a stream, open a website or connect to a server, your device connects directly to that service. Their own privacy policies apply to that connection.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
